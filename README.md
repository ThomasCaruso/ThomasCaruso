## Selected work

### [OpenAlpha](https://github.com/ThomasCaruso/OpenAlpha)
**Preregistered empirical research on financial foundation models.**

A reproducible research system for evaluating Kronos, a pretrained financial foundation model.

- SHA-256 sealed preregistrations verified inside the execution environment
- Commit-pinned execution and immutable research artifacts
- Statistical decision rules fixed before results were observed
- Three completed studies, including a 1,600-generation zero-shot benchmark across four ETFs
- Published negative results, including evidence that falsified the project's original structural-repair thesis
- Offline reproducibility and CI-enforced research-integrity checks

The central result: structural validity and forecasting skill were separable properties, and repairing invalid financial outputs did not improve forecast accuracy.
